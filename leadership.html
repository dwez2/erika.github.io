Leadership & Professional Growth

Leading Through Technology, Communication, and Continuous Improvement

I believe strong leadership is built through consistency, communication, accountability, and a commitment to continuous growth. Throughout my career in IT, cybersecurity, infrastructure, and higher education, I’ve focused on creating environments where technology supports people, operations become more efficient, and teams feel empowered to succeed.

My leadership style combines technical depth with operational awareness and emotional intelligence. Whether I am leading IT initiatives across 60+ locations, mentoring students entering cybersecurity for the first time, supporting infrastructure modernization projects, or helping organizations improve compliance and operational maturity, my goal is always the same: create clarity, reduce complexity, and build systems that are scalable, sustainable, and secure.

I’m passionate about professional development and believe great leaders never stop learning. Outside of day-to-day operations, I actively invest time into leadership books, cybersecurity research, webinars, conferences, and podcasts that help strengthen both my technical and leadership capabilities.
