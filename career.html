Driving Technology, Security, and Operational Excellence Through Leadership and Collaboration


Erika Hernandez is an IT and cybersecurity leader with experience supporting enterprise infrastructure, multi-site operations, compliance initiatives, and technical teams across healthcare, education, and business environments. Her background combines hands-on technical expertise with strategic leadership, allowing her to bridge the gap between operational execution and organizational goals.

Throughout her career, Erika has led infrastructure modernization efforts, cybersecurity initiatives, vendor management programs, and operational improvements designed to increase reliability, scalability, and security across complex IT environments. She is recognized for bringing structure to fast-moving environments while maintaining a strong focus on communication, collaboration, and long-term operational success.

A Glimpse Into My IT & Cybersecurity Journey

This section highlights projects, infrastructure improvements, teaching experiences, and technical environments that have shaped my career in IT and cybersecurity. From server room cleanups and infrastructure upgrades to classroom instruction and operational support, each project reflects a commitment to continuous improvement, problem-solving, and practical leadership.

My experience spans infrastructure management, cybersecurity operations, technical instruction, service delivery, and project coordination across healthcare, higher education, and enterprise environments. Whether supporting end users, modernizing systems, mentoring students, or strengthening operational processes, I approach every project with a focus on reliability, scalability, and long-term impact.

Cost Optimization & Vendor Management

Erika has consistently identified opportunities to reduce waste, improve vendor accountability, and align technology spending with business needs. Across multiple roles, she has reviewed cloud usage, software licensing, vendor contracts, and recurring technology expenses to find practical ways to reduce costs without weakening operations.

One example included identifying and shutting down unused AWS resources, helping reduce unnecessary monthly cloud spend. She has also supported software audits, licensing reviews, ISP consolidation, and vendor standardization efforts that improved cost visibility and strengthened long-term IT planning.

Her approach to cost optimization is not just about cutting expenses. It is about making sure technology investments support reliability, security, scalability, and business value.


Enterprise System Cleanup & Software Integration

Erika has led and supported system cleanup and integration efforts across complex IT environments, including Active Directory organization, SharePoint permission cleanup, software implementation, endpoint management, and post-acquisition technology alignment.

These projects required detailed planning, documentation, cross-functional communication, and change management to reduce disruption while improving security, usability, and operational consistency. By cleaning up outdated systems and standardizing processes, she helped create environments that were easier to manage, more secure, and better aligned with organizational needs.

This work reflects her ability to take messy or fragmented systems and turn them into more structured, reliable, and scalable technology environments.


Infrastructure Built for Reliability, Safety, and Scale

Erika has experience improving and building IT infrastructure across environments that required organization, reliability, and long-term supportability. From clinic setups and network closet improvements to access point placement, cabling coordination, and vendor-supported upgrades, she has helped create technology spaces that are safer, cleaner, and easier to maintain.

Her infrastructure work focuses on more than appearance. A well-organized IT environment improves troubleshooting, reduces downtime, supports documentation, and helps future technicians understand and maintain systems more effectively.

Whether supporting a new site, improving an existing environment, or correcting years of neglected infrastructure, Erika brings a practical, hands-on approach focused on stability, security, and operational efficiency.
