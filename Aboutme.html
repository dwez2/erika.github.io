Server Room Infrastructure Cleanup & Operational Improvement

After years of unmanaged cabling, inconsistent documentation, and IT staff turnover, Erika Hernandez helped restore structure and reliability to a disorganized server room environment. The project required both technical attention to detail and practical project coordination to assess the existing setup, identify problem areas, organize cabling, improve labeling, and create a more maintainable infrastructure space.

This work improved more than the appearance of the server room. It made troubleshooting easier, reduced confusion for future IT staff, improved visibility into connected systems, and created a cleaner foundation for ongoing infrastructure support.

This project reflects Erika’s ability to bring order to complex technical environments, document improvements clearly, and strengthen day-to-day IT operations through practical, hands-on execution.

After the cleanup, the rack became easier to understand, manage, and troubleshoot. Cables were organized, unnecessary clutter was removed, and the space became more maintainable for future IT support.
